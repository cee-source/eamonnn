HOLO-HUD

AR visor + VR arcade, built from a browser

checking device capabilities…

Phone + headset (Cardboard/WebXR): open this page on your phone, pick a mode, drop the phone in the viewer.

No headset: AR falls back to a plain camera overlay. VR falls back to drag-to-look on desktop, or tilt-to-look on phone.

AR needs camera access. VR tilt needs motion-sensor access. You'll be prompted once you enter a mode.
