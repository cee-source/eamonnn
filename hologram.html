BUILD THE PYRAMID

WHAT YOU NEED

  • Clear plastic sheet — overhead transparency, clear folder, or CD jewel case lid
  • Scissors or craft knife
  • Clear tape or super glue
  • Phone or tablet (laid flat, screen up)

CUT 4 IDENTICAL TRAPEZOIDS

Each piece is wider at the bottom, narrower at the top:

DEVICEBOTTOMTOPHEIGHT
Phone (5–6 in)6 cm1 cm3.5 cm
Tablet (10 in)10 cm1.5 cm6 cm

ASSEMBLE

  1. Cut 4 identical trapezoid pieces
  2. Tape the 4 diagonal edges together to form a pyramid shape
  3. Narrow tip points UP into the air
  4. Wide bottom sits flat on the screen

USE IT

  1. Lay phone/tablet FLAT, screen facing UP
  2. Open this page — turn screen brightness to MAX
  3. Place pyramid over the small circle in the CENTER of the screen
  4. Turn off the lights
  5. Look at the pyramid from eye level — the figure floats inside!
TIPS
Darker room = better effect. Thinner plastic (overhead transparency) works best. The hologram is most visible when you look straight at a pyramid face at eye level.
